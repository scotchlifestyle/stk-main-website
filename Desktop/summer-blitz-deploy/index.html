Official match format

One shot. One table.
No mercy. Be the last.

Killer Pool is simple. Everyone shares one table, one shot per turn. Pocket any ball to survive. Miss, scratch, or foul and you take a strike. Hit your tier's limit and you're out. The last player standing wins the match and earns +5 bonus points.

01
Player order
Order is randomized before every match. One player is randomly selected to draw a ball from the tray. The Game Runner hits shuffle that many times to set the rotation.
02
The break
Breaks must be legal — minimum 4 balls must contact a rail. Pocket a ball on the break, your turn ends. Dry break, shoot again from wherever the cue ball rests.
03
Fouls & strikes
A miss, scratch, or foul earns a strike. Scratches result in ball in hand for the next player in rotation. Elimination is determined by handicap tier, not a flat count.
04
Endgame protocol
Matches run about 1.5 hours. If too many remain after 1 hour, the rack reduces by one ball each rerack until one ball sits in the center. Last one in wins.
Shot Clock
30s to approach the table. 60s to execute the shot. 60s coaching session. 10-second call announced. Clock expiry earns a strike. Applied at Game Runner discretion to players who consistently delay play.
Coaching
Up to 5 coaches per match. The player who shoots immediately after the player being coached cannot serve as that player's coach.
Advanced
Jump shots and massés are permitted. The last ball on the table is always live. When all balls are pocketed, the table re-racks and play continues.
Out of Turn
If a player accidentally shoots out of turn, the outcome is void, all balls remain in place, and the correct player resumes their turn with ball in hand.
Rulings
Game Runner has final say. To dispute, flag immediately before play continues. Reviews are not resolved during the match. Both parties submit written accounts through the app, escalated to the Regional Manager.

Questions

Before you break.

What is Killer Pool?
A single-table format where every player shoots in rotation. One shot per turn. Pocket a ball or take a strike. Hit your tier's strike limit and you're out. Last player standing wins.
Who can play?
Any player 21 or older. STK places every player into a handicap tier based on skill, so you compete fairly regardless of experience.
What does it cost?
$50 league dues to join, and a $10 green fee per match to play. Dues are paid once your home venue hits 15 players and activates.
What is a squad?
Your squad is everyone signed to your home venue. Squad bonuses are won collectively.
Can I play at other venues?
Yes. If you miss your home venue, make up matches at any participating STK location. Playing an extra night a month at a new venue is rewarded.
How long is the season?
20 weeks per venue. Venues activate June–July once 15 players sign up, with the season completing in December. Two matches per night. Street Sharks and higher play up to 50 matches; Rookie Reapers and Smooth Shooters play up to 60.
How do I qualify for the Championship?
Top players on the leaderboard at the end of their 20-week season qualify for the STK Summer Blitz Championship in Los Angeles. Every qualifier gets a $1,000 travel stipend.
What is the prize pool?
$100,000 seasonal prize pool, building toward a $1,000,000 annual prize pool across the season's championship structure.

Know the game

Rules of the room

Shoot to Kill: Killer Pool

Players shoot in order. Player order is shuffled before the match starts. You get one shot per turn. All you have to do is make a ball - slop counts. If you miss, scratch, or foul, you get a strike. Reach your strike limit and you're eliminated. Everyone starts the game with a 3-strike limit. In this series, you can purchase up to 2 more strikes for $5 each.

Pot a ball — no strike, next player shoots

Miss, scratch, or foul — you take a strike

Once you hit your strike limit, you're eliminated.

Breaks

If you break dry, you may shoot again. If you pot at least one ball on the break, the next player shoots. Any 4 balls must make contact with a rail for the break to be legal.

Shot clock

There is a 60-second shot clock if you need coaching on a shot. Additional shot clocks for players who repeatedly slow down play: 30 seconds to approach the table, 60 seconds to shoot.

Fouls

Only cue ball fouls are counted as strikes. An object ball that comes off the table also results in a strike and remains off the table.

Conduct

Banter is welcomed. Rudeness is never tolerated.

Earn Extra Lives

Refer a new player who registers, make two separate bar purchases during a match, or eliminate Neille or any previous Kill Shot winner to earn an extra life. But if you are eliminated before one of the players with a bounty is eliminated, you do not receive an extra life. You must still be active on the scoreboard to receive the extra life bounty. These lives are added to your player account and may be saved or used during any regular Kill Shot Series match. Extra lives earned through referrals, purchases, or bounties cannot be used at the Championship. Championship extra lives can only be earned by winning qualifying matches.

Entry and Prize Money

$25 registration / $25 green fees/entry fees Split: $10 prize pool, $10 nightly cash prize, $5 admin fees Players win the total from nightly cash prize The player with the highest cumulative bar tab throughout the series receives free registration and gameplay next season.

Player Incentives

Refer new players: earn extra lives to use in any regular series match of your choosing. Eliminate Neille: earn a bounty extra life. Eliminate a previous match winner: earn a bounty extra life. Earn a bonus life for placing 2 qualifying orders per match in one night. If there are multiple matches, there are multiple opportunities to earn lives. Win a regular match: qualify for the $5000 Championship. Win multiple matches, earn Championship lives (max 2 bonus, 5 lives total). Championship lives can only be earned by winning matches. Referral, purchase, bounty rewards cannot create Championship lives.

Last player standing wins.

Frequently asked questions

First time here?

Do I have to be good at pool?

No. You get one shot per turn, and any pocketed ball counts—even slop. You’re here to play, meet people, and try to be the last one standing.

How does a match work?

Player order is shuffled before the match. Make a ball and your turn ends. Miss, scratch, or foul and you take a strike. Reach your strike limit and you’re eliminated. The last player standing wins.

How do I find a match and RSVP?

Open Matches, choose your night, and RSVP. RSVPs are for registered players and close one hour before the match starts.

What does it cost?

The current series rules list $25 registration and $25 entry per match. The entry is split into $10 for the season prize pool, $10 for the nightly cash prize, and $5 for admin.

How do I earn extra lives?

Bring a new player who registers, make two qualifying bar purchases during a match, or collect a bounty. Your profile keeps a record of earned and used regular-match credits.

What does the bounty icon mean?

Every previous Kill Shot winner is a bounty target. Neille is a bounty target too. Eliminate a target while you’re still active to earn an extra life. The next-game bounty board shows attending targets; seeded entries are marked until attendance is confirmed.

How do I qualify for the championship?

Win a regular match. A second win earns one extra championship life; a third earns another. Championship lives are capped at five: three base lives plus two bonus lives.

Can I bring my regular extra-life credits to the championship?

No. Referral, bar-purchase, and bounty credits are for regular series matches. Only qualifying match wins earn championship bonus lives.

Where do photos and match results appear?

Photos you post appear in the feed with an STK watermark. Winner cards celebrate match results, and reward announcements explain who earned an extra life and how.

How do I report a wrong stat or suggest something?

Use the feedback form. For a stats correction, include the player, match, date, and what needs fixing.
